# Code of Conduct

PASS aims to provide a respectful, constructive and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, evidence and implementation choices—not people.
- Explain disagreements with technical reasoning and relevant evidence.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different levels of experience.
- Give credit for others' work and identify sources.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation and unwanted personal contact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an available GitHub private contact channel. Do not put personal or sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports fairly and may remove content, restrict participation or take other proportionate action.

This document is a project policy and is not legal advice.
